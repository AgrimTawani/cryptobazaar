## 9-question rubric

**1. What is the app?**
India's gated peer-to-peer stablecoin exchange. Verified Indian traders buy/sell USDT and USDC against INR with on-chain smart-contract escrow — CryptoBazaar never custodies the funds.

**2. Funniest / strongest claim?**
> "We can't run away with your crypto — it's technically impossible."
And the hero rotator: **THE secure / protected / trusted P2P BAZAAR**.

**3. Visual hook?**
The site's signature **lime #D4FF00 on white/black**, in `Bebas Neue` condensed all-caps. Tactile, terminal-adjacent. Lottery-style rolling counters for live stats. Multi-chain glyphs (Polygon, Solana, Tron) as monochrome pills.

**4. What to show from the UI?**
- Hero typography with the rotating word (secure/protected/trusted)
- Stats counter — verified members, total trades, total INR volume
- "Smart Contract Escrow" pill from Step 06 of How-it-works
- Multi-chain pills: USDT (Polygon · Solana · Tron) / USDC (Polygon · Solana)
- The verification triad: Aadhaar + PAN + Liveness

**5. Shortest satisfying length?**
25s — enough to cover hook, escrow proof, and the India-specific verification angle without dragging.

**6. Tone**
- Preset: `cinematic` (anchor for pacing + restraint)
- Direction: "trader-terminal trailer — bold Bebas Neue type, lime accent stabs on black, multi-chain glyph reveal, escrow lock as a hard cinematic beat, India context (₹ → on-chain) as the emotional payoff"

**7. Audio**
- Role: cinematic bed with beat-locked reveals

- SFX: 1× soft impact on hero word reveal, 3× soft UI ticks on chain pills, 1× hard lock click on escrow, 1× announcement chime on outro

**8. Share caption (draft)**

**9. User flow worth showing**
Sign up + verify (Aadhaar/PAN/Liveness) → Pick an offer in the marketplace → Smart-contract escrow locks the crypto → Pay INR bank-to-bank → Auto-release.

## Creative angle

CryptoBazaar isn't another exchange. It's an *anti-exchange*: instead of holding your funds, it locks them in a public smart contract while you pay rupees directly to another verified human. The brag video plays this as a tense, cinematic reveal — "you've seen exchanges, you've never seen this" — using the site's lime/black brand to land each promise as a stamp. Cinematic restraint with two chaotic accelerations (chain reveal, verification triad) to keep it from feeling corporate.
